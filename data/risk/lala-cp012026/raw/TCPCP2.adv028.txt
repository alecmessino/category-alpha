DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 1100 PM HST (0900 UTC), the center of Hurricane Lala was located
near latitude 20.9 North, longitude 169.7 West.  Lala is moving
toward the northwest near 9 mph (15 km/h).  This general motion is
expected for about a day, followed by a turn towards the
north early on Thursday.  On the forecast track, the center of Lala
will be near the northwestern Hawaiian Islands on Thursday.
 
Satellite data indicate that the maximum sustained winds are near
130 mph (215 km/h) with higher gusts.  Lala is a category 4
hurricane on the Saffir-Simpson Hurricane Wind Scale.  Some gradual
weakening is anticipated during the next few days, but Lala is
expected to remain a hurricane.
  
Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 30 miles (45 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 115 miles
(185 km).
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 947 mb (27.97 inches).
 
 
HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
WIND: Hurricane conditions are expected in the Hurricane Warning
area by Thursday, with tropical storm conditions expected by
Wednesday night. Tropical storm conditions are expected in the
Tropical Storm Warning area by early Thursday.
 
SURF: Large surf associated with Lala will affect the
Papahanaumokuakea Marine National Monument beginning early
Wednesday. These large waves will likely inundate some of the
low-lying atolls, especially from French Frigate Shoals to Maro
Reef.
