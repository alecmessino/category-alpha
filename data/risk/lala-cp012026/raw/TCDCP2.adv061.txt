Tropical Storm Lala Discussion Number  61
NWS Central Pacific Hurricane Center Honolulu HI   CP012026
Issued by NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
500 AM HST Thu Aug 27 2026
 
Convection associated with Lala is gradually decreasing, with the 
central convection comprised of some weak bands to the east of the 
center. However, ASCAT and OSCAT scatterometer data indicate that 
the maximum winds remain near 40 kt.

The cyclone is moving northward or 350/4 kt.  A northwestward motion 
is expected for the next day or so, followed by a turn toward the 
west-northwest as Lala becomes embedded in the flow between a 
low-level ridge to the north and a western Pacific tropical cyclone 
approaching from the south. This motion should continue until the 
system dissipates. The new forecast track is similar to the previous 
track.

A combination of cool sea surface temperatures, moderate shear, and 
dry air entrainment should cause Lala to weaken, and the cyclone is 
expected to lose its organized convection and become post-tropical 
between 24-36 h. The remnants of Lala are forecast to weaken to a 
trough between 60-72 h.
