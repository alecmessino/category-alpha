...400 PM HST (0200 UTC) POSITION UPDATE...

Radar data indicate that the eyewall of Lala is nearing South Point, 
Hawaii.

An observation near South Point around 550 feet elevation recently 
reported a sustained wind of 51 mph (82 km/h) and a gust of 71 mph 
(114 km/h). An observation site at South Point Park also reported a 
sustained wind of 49 mph (79 km/h) and a gust of 73 mph (117 km/h).

Peak rainfall measurements over the last 12 hours are between 12 to 
13 inches across windward Maui and the Big Island.
