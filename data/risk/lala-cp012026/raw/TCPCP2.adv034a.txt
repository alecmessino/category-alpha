DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 200 PM HST (0000 UTC), the center of Hurricane Lala was located 
near latitude 25.2 North, longitude 172.4 West. Lala is moving 
toward the north near 10 mph (17 km/h). A northwestward motion is 
expected by this weekend. On the forecast track, the center of Lala 
will continue to traverse the Papahanaumokuakea Marine National 
Monument for the next couple of days.
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 90 mph (150 km/h) with higher 
gusts. Although some intensity fluctuations are possible over the 
next day or so, a gradual weakening trend should begin by the 
weekend, though Lala is forecast to remain a hurricane throughout 
that time.
 
Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 45 miles (75 km) from
the center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 175
miles (280 km).
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 973 mb (28.74 inches).
 
 
HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
WIND: Hurricane conditions are expected in the Hurricane Warning
area today, with tropical storm conditions ongoing. Tropical
storm conditions are ongoing in the Tropical Storm Warning area.
 
SURF: Large surf associated with Lala will affect the 
Papahanaumokuakea Marine National Monument for the next couple of 
days. These large waves will likely inundate some of the low-lying 
atolls, especially from French Frigate Shoals to Maro Reef.
