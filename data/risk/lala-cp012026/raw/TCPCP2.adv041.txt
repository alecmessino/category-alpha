DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 500 AM HST (1500 UTC), the center of Hurricane Lala was located
near latitude 30.5 North, longitude 172.4 West. Lala is moving
toward the northwest near 10 mph (17 km/h). This motion is 
expected to continue at a slower speed during the next few days.
 
Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 80 mph (130 km/h) 
with higher gusts. Gradual weakening is forecast during the next 
several days, and Lala's winds could decrease below hurricane force 
by early Sunday.
 
Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 60 miles (95 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 255 miles
(405 km).
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 979 mb (28.91 inches).
 
 
HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
SURF: Large surf associated with Lala will affect western sections 
of the Papahanaumokuakea Marine National Monument for the next 
couple of days. These large waves could inundate some low-lying
atolls, especially from Maro Reef to Kure Atoll.
