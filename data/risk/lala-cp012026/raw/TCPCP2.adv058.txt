DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 1100 AM HST (2100 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Lala was
located near latitude 36.1 North, longitude 178.3 West. Lala is
moving toward the northwest near 5 mph (7 km/h), with a turn toward 
the north-northwest expected through Thursday. 
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts.

forecast to become a post-tropical remnant low by Thursday night. 
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 115 miles (185 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 999 mb (29.50 inches).
