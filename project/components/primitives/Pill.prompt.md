**Pill** — rounded capsule chip used for the strategy chip, storm selectors, and imagery-product toggles. `active` gives an accent fill + ring glow; `dotColor` adds a leading status/phase dot.

```jsx
<Pill>Category Alpha</Pill>
<Pill active dotColor="var(--pai-velocity)" onClick={pick}>Bertha C3</Pill>
<Pill mono={false}>GeoColor</Pill>
```
