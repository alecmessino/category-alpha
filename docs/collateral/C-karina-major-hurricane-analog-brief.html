The interval on that Cat 4 row runs 1-31% on a denominator of fourteen — too wide to carry a rarity claim. SO WHAT — RESEARCH USE, NOT THREAT MONITORING

Karina has already passed both climb milestones this cohort times; the timings printed above are history, not a schedule for her. What the cohort does support is where she stands: above every printed member of her own genesis cell, on a fourteen-storm denominator whose only scoreable land exposure is Mexico, whose Mexico — ≥64 kt row is a zero, and whose other ten land contracts the archive declines to score with a stated reason each. That is the frame this page gives a researcher or a client-facing analyst — a stated denominator, an interval on every rate, and the refusals visible next to them. Nothing here is threat monitoring, a forecast, a track or a landfall claim about Karina. The archive scores genesis-conditioned landfall rows for the cohort, printed above; it scores no row about where a live storm goes, and this brief does not manufacture one. No price call, no trade view, no skill claim.

RESEARCH ONLY — NOT A FORECAST. Storm Atlas is a historical genesis/outcome engine. Every rate on this page is GENESIS-CONDITIONED: it assumes a tropical cyclone forms and describes what the historical record did next. It is not P(forms), not a live feed, and not a forecast cone. To combine with a formation probability: P(reaches X) = P(forms) × P(reaches X | forms). Landfall does not decompose that way and is counted jointly, never as a product of marginals.
