Storm Atlas

A research-only historical genesis/outcome engine for the North Atlantic and East Pacific: give it a genesis point or a declared pre-genesis reference cell and it returns what the record did next as exact n/N with a 95% Wilson interval, names every storm in the denominator, and refuses what the record cannot carry.

01

What a user can do

  • Condition on a point. A genesis position or a declared pre-genesis reference cell, with a stated radius and month window. Rates assume formation — not P(forms), not a live feed, not a cone.
  • Retrieve the analog tracks. Every storm in the cohort, and representative members under a stated rule with genesis date, distance from the cell, peak and their own landfalls. Nothing is ranked a closest analog.
  • Compare contract rows. Reached TD, TS and Cat 1–5, each exact n/N with a 95% Wilson interval; six landfall regions, each an “any” and a ≥64 kt pair. State-level landfall is not scored.
  • Read the refusals as output. Below min sample: counts, no rate, no interval. Move the cell and watch N and the refusal move with it.

THE MOAT — SANITATION, REFUSAL, PROVENANCE

An interpolated fix never establishes a threshold crossing, so time-to-hurricane is never dated off a wind nobody measured. A cohort’s defining variable is not reported back as an outcome of it, with one exception left in the open: every cohort returns reached TD at 100%, because a cohort is storms that formed. Below min sample a rate is refused rather than estimated. An absent outcome is not a zero. Every figure carries a pack stamp and a URL that reproduces it.

03

Delivery, and what Storm Atlas adds

CURRENT — SHIPPING TODAY

A browser and replayable cohort URLs. A URL carries the cell, radius, season floor, month window, basin and methodology version, so a reviewer opens the same cohort and reads the same counts, rates, intervals and refusals.

PROPOSED / PILOT — NOT SHIPPING

PROPOSED / PILOT — not available today. Exportable charts. An API. Recurring institutional briefs. None of the three exists yet.

RESEARCH ONLY — NOT A FORECAST. Every rate here is GENESIS-CONDITIONED: it assumes formation and describes what the record did next — not P(forms), not a live feed, not a cone. AN UNCONDITIONAL INTENSITY PROBABILITY REQUIRES AN EXTERNAL FORMATION PROBABILITY DEFINED ON THE SAME FORMATION EVENT AND CONDITIONING SET. None is computed here. An NHC outlook probability is not multiplied by these Atlas rows unless the conditioning events are demonstrably aligned. Landfall is counted jointly and is never decomposed into path/intensity marginals.
