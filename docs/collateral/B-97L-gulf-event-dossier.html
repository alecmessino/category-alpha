WHAT THE ARCHIVE REFUSES ON THIS COHORT

  • hawaii:any — OUT OF SCOPE -- unscoreable here. only 0 storm(s) in the NA basin since 1971 carry this outcome, below the 10 distinct events any skill score requires. The archive holds 11 in total, outside the population this query draws from. Widen the basin or the era and this contract becomes scoreable; a skill number over a population that does not carry the events would be borrowed from one that does.
  • hawaii:hurricane — BASE RATE ONLY -- unscoreable. only 2 storm(s) in the entire archive carry this outcome, below the 10 distinct events any skill score requires. A base rate can be quoted with its interval; a calibrated or skill-scored probability cannot, and this archive will not produce one.

These stamps are the archive's own strings, reproduced character for character. A stamped row still publishes its count and its interval; what it will not carry is a calibrated or skill-scored probability.

09

What Storm Atlas adds

The question a desk actually asks NHC / Zoom Earth / public maps Storm Atlas
Where is it now, and how strong?The observation: position, intensity, pressure, motion — now.Silent. Storm Atlas holds no live view and makes none.
Where will it go?The forecast cone and official track guidance, 5 days.Silent, by construction. No cone, no track forecast, no skill claim.
Will it form at all?P(forms) — the graphical outlook's 48 h and 7 d chance.Silent. Every rate here is conditional on formation and says nothing about it.
What happened to storms that began here before?Not answered. No cohort, no denominator, no interval.Exact n / N with a 95% Wilson interval on every contract row, from a declared genesis point or pre-genesis reference cell, with the analog tracks drawn.
How good is the evidence, and where does it run out?Not answered.Visible refusal. Effective sample size, the min-sample gate, the archive-wide event gate, and the pre-satellite bias warning — printed beside the number, or instead of it.
Can a counterparty reproduce it?The advisory is public; the reasoning behind a number is not reproducible from it.Yes. The cohort is a URL. Same question, same pack stamp, same numbers, by anyone.
