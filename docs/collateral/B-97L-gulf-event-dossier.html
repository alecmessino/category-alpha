COMMERCIAL RELEVANCE — INTERPRETATION, NOT MEASUREMENT

Insured-region adjacency. The rows below are CONUS-wide, not Gulf-coast rows. The archive scores no sub-CONUS region, and this cohort's crossings include Florida landfalls alongside Texas and Louisiana ones, so against a Gulf-facing book they are a national-coastline prior, not a Gulf number. 10 of 12 crossed CONUS at any intensity (83.3%, 95% Wilson 55-95%); 1 of 12 crossed at ≥64 kt (8.3%, 95% Wilson 1-35%). The second row is a subset of the first: in this cohort a crossing was ordinary and a hurricane-force crossing was one storm. An attachment set at hurricane force is being informed by a single archived event with a 1-35% band around it. Use it to bound or to challenge a rate set elsewhere; do not derive one from it. Energy adjacency. The reference cell sits in north-central Gulf production and marine-logistics geography. The archive's transit to a CONUS crossing is a median 65.5 h (n=22, p25 36.75, p75 176.25) — and n=22 counts recorded landfall rows, not storms: 10 of the 12 members crossed, and FERN 1971 supplies 12 of the 22 rows.
