COMMERCIAL RELEVANCE — INTERPRETATION, NOT MEASUREMENT

  • Insured-region adjacency. These rows are CONUS-wide, not Gulf-coast: the archive scores no sub-CONUS region, and crossings include Florida as well as Texas and Louisiana. 10 of 12 crossed at any intensity (83.3%, 55-95%); 1 of 12 at ≥64 kt (8.3%, 1-35%). A hurricane-force attachment rests on one archived event. Bound or challenge a rate set elsewhere; do not derive one.
  • Energy adjacency and timing. Transit to a CONUS crossing is a median 65.5 h, its upper quartile largely one 1971 track re-counted.
  • BASIS RISK. ANITA 1977 formed 126 km from this cell and struck Tamaulipas; ALICIA 1983 formed 194 km from it and struck Texas — one cell, two coasts. Peak is not landfall intensity: FERN 1971 peaked at 80 kt, its Louisiana crossings 25 kt.

THE HOLES, PRESERVED

Refused by the instrument. No Hawaii rate: hawaii:any OUT OF SCOPE for a North Atlantic cohort; hawaii:hurricane BASE RATE ONLY, 2 events archive-wide against 10. No rates at all at 150 km, where N=5.

No genesis point. The archive returns none for AL052026: no cohort here is keyed to this storm’s formation.

Out of scope. No state-level landfall row, and no unconditional number — that would need an external formation probability on the same formation event and conditioning set.

WHAT ATLAS ADDS. A public map gives position, P(forms) and a cone; Atlas gives exact n / N with a 95% Wilson interval on every contract row, a visible refusal where the record runs out, and a URL that reproduces both.
