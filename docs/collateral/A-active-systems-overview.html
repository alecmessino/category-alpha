02 · What does Storm Atlas add?
Historical tracks near a point are already public — NOAA will draw them from the same IBTrACS this archive is built on. What is not public is the answer returned as a scored contract: a declared cell and radius, an exact n/N, the 95% Wilson interval the engine returned, the named cohort, a citation string and a replayable URL. Where the record is short the row comes back stamped — OUT OF SCOPE, BASE RATE ONLY, or RATE REFUSED — so the refusal arrives as an output rather than as a gap.
03 · How does this help price, hedge, structure, explain, distribute?
For 97L's declared cell, conditioned on formation having already occurred and not combinable with the 70% development outlook — landfall is counted jointly, never as a product of marginals: CONUS — any is 10 of 12 (83.3%, 95% Wilson 55-95%), a forty-point interval around something a Gulf underwriter already assumes; the row carrying information is CONUS ≥64 kt, 1 of 12 (8.3%, 95% Wilson 1-35%), one event. Both are 250 km results — tighten the same cell to 150 km and the cohort falls to 5 against a minimum of 10 and every rate refuses, CONUS included. A desk sets the n/N and its interval beside a broker's or a counterparty's landfall assumption and reads whether that assumption sits inside the interval, cites the URL in the pricing note so the query is replayable, requeries once a centre is fixed, and where a row is stamped says stamped rather than substituting judgement; genesis-to-TS at median 21 h (n=10, p25 7.5 h, p75 33 h) is a cohort clock, not an addition to the 48h outlook. What ships today is the query, the cohort, the refusal stamp and the citation string — on this page the stamp fires on one system entirely and on ten of twelve landfall contracts for two others; exportable charts, an API and recurring institutional briefs are PROPOSED / PILOT.

What Storm Atlas adds

and where it is deliberately silent
The question a desk actually asks NHC / Zoom Earth / public maps Storm Atlas
Where is it now, and how strong?The observation: position, intensity, pressure, motion — now.Silent. Storm Atlas holds no live view and makes none.
Where will it go?The forecast cone and official track guidance, 5 days.Silent, by construction. No cone, no track forecast, no skill claim.
Will it form at all?P(forms) — the graphical outlook's 48 h and 7 d chance.Silent. Every rate here is conditional on formation and says nothing about it.
What happened to storms that began here before?Not answered. No cohort, no denominator, no interval.Exact n / N with a 95% Wilson interval on every contract row, from a declared genesis point or pre-genesis reference cell, with the analog tracks drawn.
How good is the evidence, where does it run out, and can a counterparty reproduce it?Not answered. The advisory is public; the reasoning behind a number is not reproducible from it.Visible refusal — effective sample size, the min-sample gate, the archive-wide event gate and the pre-satellite bias warning, printed beside the number or instead of it. And the cohort is a URL: same question, same pack stamp, same numbers, by anyone.
