02

Trigger explainability, refusal, near misses

WHY A REPLAYABLE COHORT IS THE ARTEFACT

A parametric trigger’s basis-risk file needs a prior a counterparty can reopen, not a number in a deck. The cohort is a URL: cell, radius, month window, season floor and methodology version travel in it, so the same query returns the same n/N and interval on any desk, and the denominator is named.

Cohort research, trigger design and counterparty explanation — not live pricing.

THE REFUSAL A COUNTERPARTY CAN CHECK

Tighten the same question from 250 km to 150 km and the cohort falls to N = 5. Every rate then REFUSES: the engine returns “5 storms with a known outcome < min_sample=10” on every row. Counts still publish — CONUS any 4 of 5 — because a count is a fact and a rate over 5 storms is not. That refusal has its own replay URL below.

BASIS RISK — READ BEFORE USING ANY ROW ABOVE

  • Same genesis cell ≠ same landfall. Twelve storms formed inside one 250 km circle and went to Texas, Louisiana, Florida and Tamaulipas.
  • Same class ≠ same local outcome. The archive scores a crossing and its intensity, not a footprint or a loss.
  • A CONUS row is not a state row. No state-level landfall is scored, at any radius or sample size.
  • The interval is the precision. 1 of 12 is 8.3%, 95% Wilson 1-35%.

NEAR MISSES. Anita 1977 formed 126 km from the cell and struck Tamaulipas at 120 kt — inside the cohort, outside the CONUS row. Alicia 1983 formed 194 km out and struck Texas at 100 kt: the single member behind CONUS — ≥64 kt, and at 150 km not a member. Barry 2019 formed 110 km out with a 65 kt Louisiana landfall, but its 11 July genesis is outside the window.
