THE PUBLISHED QUESTION — CONDITIONAL ON FORMATION

If a tropical cyclone were to form within 250 km of 28.0°N 88.7°W — the declared pre-genesis reference cell — in August or September, in seasons from 1971 onwards, what historically happened to storms that formed there? SUFFICIENT, N = 12, ESS 12, min sample 10. It says nothing about whether the system forms — P(forms) is NHC's outlook number, not Storm Atlas's; the two compose by multiplication for intensity thresholds and do not compose at all for landfall, which the archive counts jointly.

02 · What does Storm Atlas add?
The alternative is not a public map but your own IBTrACS or HURDAT2 query, which a cat modelling team can write. Storm Atlas adds the declared cohort, exact n/N with 95% Wilson intervals, printed refusals, a replay URL.
03 · How does this help price, hedge, structure, explain, distribute?
It gives a basis-risk file one component that can be tested: a cohort the counterparty reopens from a URL and reproduces line for line, misses included. Use it upstream of pricing, for trigger design and counterparty explanation. It sets no price and forecasts nothing.

02

Trigger explainability

and the refusal a counterparty can check

WHY A REPLAYABLE COHORT IS THE ARTEFACT

A parametric trigger’s basis-risk file needs a prior a counterparty can reopen, not a number in a deck. The cohort is a URL: cell, radius, month window, season floor and methodology version travel in it, so the same query returns the same n/N, the same interval and the same stamps on any desk.

The denominator is named. Twelve storms, each listed, each with its own recorded landfalls — a reviewer can check the count rather than accept it.

WHERE THIS SITS — RESEARCH, NOT LIVE PRICING

Cohort research, trigger-threshold design and counterparty explanation. Not live pricing of a running event: Storm Atlas holds no live view, and every rate here is conditional on a formation it does not forecast.

THE REFUSAL A COUNTERPARTY CAN CHECK

Tighten the same question from 250 km to 150 km and the cohort falls to N = 5. Every rate then REFUSES: the engine returns “5 storms with a known outcome < min_sample=10” on every row rather than a rate computed over 5 storms. Counts are still published — CONUS any 4 of 5 — because a count is a fact and a rate over 5 storms is not one. That refusal has its own replay URL, cited below.

BASIS RISK — READ BEFORE USING ANY ROW ABOVE

  • Same genesis cell ≠ same landfall. Twelve storms formed inside one 250 km circle and went to Texas, Louisiana, Florida and Tamaulipas.
  • Same class ≠ same local outcome. The archive scores a crossing and its intensity, not a footprint, a surge or a loss.
  • A CONUS row is not a state row. No state-level landfall is scored, at any radius or sample size. None is supplied here.
  • The interval is the precision. 1 of 12 is 8.3% with a 95% Wilson band of 1-35%. The band is the finding; the point estimate is not.
  • A refused row is not a zero. At 150 km the same question returns RATE REFUSED, and a stamped row’s 0.0% is voided by its stamp.

SELECTION RULE — up to 8 members — majors (≥96 kt) first, then hurricanes (≥64 kt), then by peak wind descending, ties by season descending. Not a similarity ranking: Storm Atlas implements no closest-analog metric for a filter-defined cohort, and every member is weighted 1.0. Printed 8 of 12 members carrying a peak-wind value. A landfall line is a fact about that storm, sub-region included; a member's landfall is not a rate and no sub-region here is scored anywhere in this package. ×n marks repeat crossings of one coast, collapsed to the strongest.

A near-miss is a cohort member that satisfies the genesis rule and then fails the condition a trigger would be written on.

  • Anita 1977. Formed 126 km from the cell; ashore in Tamaulipas, Mexico at 120 kt. Inside the cohort, outside the CONUS row.
  • Alicia 1983. Formed 194 km out, ashore in Texas at 100 kt — the single member behind CONUS — ≥64 kt at 1 of 12 (8.3%, 95% Wilson 1-35%). That is a CONUS row, not a Texas one: the archive does not score state-level landfall. At 150 km Alicia is not a member at all, and every rate refuses.
  • Barry 2019. Formed 110 km from the cell with a 65 kt Louisiana landfall, but its 11 July genesis falls outside August–September: not in this cohort. It is the closest thing in the record to the event a trigger here would be written for. Read the misses first.

Every rate on this page is GENESIS-CONDITIONED: it assumes a tropical cyclone forms and describes what the historical record did next. It is not P(forms), not a live feed, and not a forecast cone.
