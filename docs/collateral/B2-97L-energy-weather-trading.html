03

Before these rows are quoted

adjacency, never impact

Geography is not a probability. Landfall resolves to region, not state: CONUS, Mexico, Caribbean, Central America, Hawaii and unattributed, each an “any” and a “>=64 kt” form. Both Hawaii rows are refused here — OUT OF SCOPE and BASE RATE ONLY. There is no Texas, Louisiana or Gulf-state row to quote.

What this page is not. No prices, no trades, no view on gas, power, refined products or freight. No state rates. Every row is genesis-conditioned and assumes formation: no unconditional number is computed here, and no NHC outlook probability is multiplied by these rows — the composition rule is in the footer. Landfall is counted jointly, never decomposed.
