Geography, exposure and the refusals

adjacency, never impact

READ THIS BEFORE THE PLATE

Landfall resolves to region, not state: CONUS, Mexico, Caribbean, Central America, Hawaii and unattributed, each an “any” and a “>=64 kt” form. Both Hawaii rows are refused here — OUT OF SCOPE and BASE RATE ONLY. There is no Texas, Louisiana or Gulf-state row to quote. The paths on the plate are geometry, not a rate.

EXPOSURE CLASSES ADJACENT TO THIS COHORT

Two hurricane landfalls, opposite sides of the basin. CONUS — >=64 kt is 1 of 12 (8.3%, 1-35%); MEXICO — >=64 kt is 1 of 12 (8.3%, 1-35%). ANITA 1977 is the Mexican one, Tamaulipas at 120 kt; ALICIA 1983 the CONUS one, Texas at 100 kt. The rates are region rows — a CONUS row, not a Texas one.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS NOT

No prices, no trades, no view on gas, power, refined products or freight. No state rates. Every row is genesis-conditioned and assumes formation: no unconditional number is computed here, and no NHC outlook probability is multiplied by these rows — the composition rule is in the footer. Landfall is counted jointly, never decomposed.

WHAT ATLAS ADDS. A public map gives position, P(forms) and a cone; Atlas gives exact n / N with a 95% Wilson interval on every contract row, a visible refusal where the record runs out, and a URL that reproduces both.

RESEARCH ONLY — NOT A FORECAST. Every rate here is GENESIS-CONDITIONED: it assumes formation and describes what the record did next — not P(forms), not a live feed, not a cone. AN UNCONDITIONAL INTENSITY PROBABILITY REQUIRES AN EXTERNAL FORMATION PROBABILITY DEFINED ON THE SAME FORMATION EVENT AND CONDITIONING SET. None is computed here. An NHC outlook probability is not multiplied by these Atlas rows unless the conditioning events are demonstrably aligned. Landfall is counted jointly and is never decomposed into path/intensity marginals.
