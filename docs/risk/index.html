MethodologyStanding commitments

The rules every record on this page is built under

Across the 2 settled events listed above: 396 archived official products and 402 hashed inputs. A rebuild of either record is byte-identical to what is published here, and that is checked rather than claimed.

These are not aspirations. Each one is enforced by a gate that runs on every change, and each was written because something went wrong without it.

Three times, never derived from one another

A forecast has a nominal cycle (the synoptic hour it belongs to), an issuance and a valid time, and they are stored separately. Forecast lead is valid − nominal_cycle and nothing else. Reading the release hour as forecast hour zero is what produced the residual this work retired, and the proof is documentary: the discussion products print the forecast table with its lead labels attached, and those labels reconcile against the cycle and against nothing else. Both hypotheses are scored on every archived discussion; the cycle origin holds on all of them and the initial-position origin on none.

A source vintage is carried, not smoothed over

Where two official products report the same fix differently, both are published as peers and neither overwrites the other. Where an archive establishes issuance only to the hour, no minute is invented to make it match an archive that has one.

Every input declared, and every declaration read

The list of files a build opens is the list its manifest hashes — checked in both directions, because the failure that prompted it had both halves: a declared input that was never shipped, and the geometry it actually read appearing nowhere in its own register.

A refusal is a result

A rate without a denominator is not a small rate. An ambiguous cycle is not resolved by convention. A settlement that public terms cannot reproduce is reported as not reconstructable, with the missing terms named. Nothing here infers a contract term from an outcome.

Nothing on this page prices anything

These records are evidence about data, vintage, geometry, units and contract version. They are not a forecast, a loss estimate or a claims determination, and no page reachable from here quotes, ranks or prices a market. The gate that enforces it does not take the word for it: it fails on the vocabulary of such a surface appearing anywhere on this page at all.
